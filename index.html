About Adalah Council

عَدَالَة ʿadālah — justice, balance, equity

Adalah Council is a nonprofit research and education organization founded in 2025 to bring qualified religious scholarship to the questions that shape public life. Its name — ʿadālah, the Arabic word for justice and balance — reflects a conviction that sound guidance rests on both fidelity to the Islamic intellectual tradition and careful attention to the realities of the modern world.

The Council serves individuals, families, and institutions seeking thoughtful, principled answers to contemporary ethical, legal, and social questions. It pursues this through research and publication, public education, and advisory guidance grounded in scholarship rather than personality or partisanship.

We approach these questions with intellectual seriousness and independence. Adalah Council welcomes a range of perspectives, resists political alignment, and holds that religious scholarship serves the public best when it is rigorous, transparent, and accountable to the tradition it represents.

Programs & activities

Our work spans four areas. As lectures, publications, statements, and initiatives are developed, they will be announced within these areas.

Educational Programming

Public lectures, seminars, and workshops that make serious scholarship accessible, alongside educational resources for continued study.

Research & Publications

Articles, essays, and applied research on the ethical and legal questions facing Muslims today, written for both scholarly and public audiences.

Advisory Initiatives

Research-informed guidance and consultation for individuals, families, and institutions navigating complex contemporary questions.

Community Engagement

Public programming and dialogue that connect scholarship to the concerns of Northern Virginia's Muslim community and the broader public.
